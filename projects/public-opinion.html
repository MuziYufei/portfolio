Public Opinion

群眾意見

A party game centered on spoken answers, identity labels, and group judgment.

Context
Independent tabletop study · 4–8 players
Media
Cards · tokens · forum and draw pool
Site
Online discussion forum · tabletop setting

From answers to labels: how does a group judge a person?

Online discussions often compress complex positions into a single label. Public Opinion brings that mechanism to the table: one player responds to a public prompt, while the other players privately choose the identity card that best describes the speaker.

Label choices and a consensus threshold determine the score, allowing the game to explore agreement and social pressure within a group. Differences revealed by the cards also give players something to discuss.

Forum discussion and online card draws.

Players can post everyday disputes, respond to other viewpoints, and draw questions to use in the tabletop game.

New questions can enter the draw pool and expand the game’s topic library. The mechanism is intended to attract players through a wider range of topics, with their posts and replies contributing further questions.
